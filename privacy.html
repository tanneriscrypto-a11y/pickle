Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026

PICKLE is a personal assistant used only by its owner. This policy explains how it handles Google user data.

Data accessed

With the owner's permission, PICKLE reads the owner's Gmail messages using the read-only Gmail scope (gmail.readonly). It cannot send, delete, or change email.

How the data is used

Email content is used only to answer the owner's own requests, such as finding or summarizing a message. It is processed on the owner's computer by a local AI model and is not sent to any other AI service. PICKLE's answers are delivered to the owner through the owner's own private Telegram chat.

Storage

Email content is not kept beyond the conversation in which it was requested, apart from local logs on the owner's computer. Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on that computer.

Sharing

Google user data is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Limited Use

PICKLE's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke PICKLE's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Open an issue on GitHub.
