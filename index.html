PICKLE

Personal Intelligence Coordinating Key Life Errands

A private home assistant with exactly one user.

PICKLE runs on its owner's personal computer and helps with everyday errands: finding and summarizing the owner's own email, reminders, household information, and handing larger jobs to Claude Code.

Not a public service. PICKLE has no sign-up and no other users. It exists to help the person who built it.

Private by design. Email and other personal data are read on the owner's computer by a local AI model and never sent to outside AI services.
